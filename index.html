Who Should Join

Students

Undergraduate, Master's, or PhD students in Biochemistry, Molecular Biology, Biotechnology, Pharmacy, Genetic Engineering or any lifescience disciplinary field.

Early-Career Researchers

Anyone wanting hands-on computational biology exposure beyond wet-lab work.

Thesis-Track Students

Those preparing thesis or manuscript work who need CADD, network pharmacology, CAVD, RNA-Seq, or cancer bioinformatics skills.

Curious Beginners

Basic biology background and curiosity is enough — no prior coding experience required.

Not a fit if you're looking for a purely theoretical course with no hands-on components.

Curriculum Roadmap

Seven modules, each ending in a real deliverable.

  • Introduction to bioinformatics: scope and applications in drug discovery
  • Biological data types: DNA, RNA, and protein sequences
  • Sequence data retrieval and database overview
  • Key bioinformatics tools and workflows for molecular analysis
Deliverable: Annotated sequence/database report
  • Drug discovery pipeline and target identification
  • Structure-based and ligand-based drug design principles
  • Protein structure analysis and chemical representation
  • Ligand preparation and chemical structure drawing
  • Drug-likeness evaluation and physicochemical profiling
  • ADME and toxicity prediction
  • Protein structure modeling, AlphaFold prediction, and model validation
  • Pharmacophore modeling for lead identification
  • Molecular docking and protein–ligand interaction analysis
  • Interpretation of docking results
  • Molecular dynamics and flexibility analysis of protein–ligand complexes
Deliverable: Docking + ADMET report on a candidate compound
  • Concepts of network and systems pharmacology
  • Multitarget drug discovery strategies
  • Identification of bioactive compounds from natural sources
  • Prediction of compound–target interactions and disease-associated targets
  • Protein–protein interaction (PPI) network construction and analysis
  • Hub target identification using network topology
  • Functional enrichment (Gene Ontology) and pathway (KEGG) analysis
  • Construction of drug–target, target–pathway, and integrated drug–target–pathway networks
  • Docking validation of key targets
Deliverable: Compound-disease-target network diagram + pathway report
  • Build and optimize molecular structures
  • Perform DFT calculations using Gaussian
  • Analyze HOMO-LUMO
  • Perform IR, Raman & NMR calculations
  • Interpret computational results
  • Prepare DFT results for research publications
Deliverable: DFT analysis report with spectral interpretation
  • Antigen selection & epitope prediction (B-cell, T-cell/MHC-I/MHC-II)
  • Multi-epitope construct design (linkers, adjuvants)
  • Physicochemical/allergenicity/antigenicity evaluation
  • 3D structure modeling & docking with immune receptors
  • Immune simulation (C-ImmSim)
Deliverable: Multi-epitope vaccine construct workflow report
  • Cancer genomics databases (TCGA, GEO, cBioPortal)
  • Differential gene expression basics
  • Survival analysis (Kaplan-Meier), biomarker identification
  • Pathway/functional enrichment for cancer datasets
Deliverable: Biomarker/expression analysis mini-report
  • Research paper structure & IMRaD
  • Literature review & research gap identification
  • Scientific & academic writing
  • Introduction, Methods, Results & Discussion
  • Computational results writing
  • Tables, figures & scientific data presentation
  • Citation & reference management
  • Plagiarism & research ethics
  • Journal selection & manuscript formatting
  • Cover letter & reviewer response
  • Guided manuscript writing project
Deliverable: Draft manuscript section based on internship project
